# Analysis review

- Are claims traceable to source rows and inspected figures? Are numerical units,
  missing values, precision, exclusions and plotting axes described correctly?
- Were independent syntheses distinguished from specimens and processing variants?
- Are correlation, causal interpretation and uncertainty separated? Were operator
  material changes and warm-start provenance considered?
- Is the prior prediction evaluated using its declared criteria rather than just
  improvement? Are alternative explanations and absent evidence visible?

  incorporated and no fabricated visual/model/experimental execution?
