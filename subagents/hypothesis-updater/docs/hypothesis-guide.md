# Hypothesis updates

- Ground each hypothesis in observations and state a testable explanation.
  Include competing explanations when existing data cannot distinguish them.
- Keep stable IDs; retain old hypotheses and mark status as proposed, supported,
  refuted or inconclusive. Supported means bounded evidence, not proven truth.
- Explain previous status, new status, evidence and revision reason in the report.
  A new mechanism gets a new ID; do not silently rewrite an old claim.
- Cite condition/source identities. Preserve missing evidence and uncertainty.
  Do not force a new question, an exploration choice or a new hypothesis each round.
