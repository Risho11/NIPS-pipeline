# Developing useful explanations and preserving their meaning

Use this guide with the [hypothesis-updater role](../AGENTS.md), shared
[data contract](../../../docs/mas/data-contract.md),
[knowledge contract](../../../docs/mas/knowledge.md) and
[workflow](../../../docs/mas/workflow.md). Main selects the test after independent
review; this guide neither executes a condition nor changes invocation permissions.
Default explicit dispatch integrates analysis and explanation here, using shared
[analysis methods](../../../docs/mas/analysis-methods.md); split work follows main's
bounded duties and review coverage, without making prior analysis a universal gate.

## Start from an unresolved question, not a mechanism label

For a new bank, begin with allowed theory/source and material/process/measurement
context before current outcomes, without requiring current reviewed analysis.
Preserve initial meaning, direction, prerequisites and credible rival predictions
before reveal; give fresh participants no old agent hypotheses/reports, worked
causal answers or private outcomes. Use approved outcome-free definitions/context.
Account for important tunables, fixed settings, constants and environmental/provenance
context in the relevant topic documents or linked context, with reasoned coverage
or explicit unlinked reasons/cues.
Cover process stages/interactions without a one-variable-per-claim catalog.
For recurring updates, read relevant bank records and authorized observations;
ask which relationship matters and what remains unexplained. Do not re-bootstrap
or read the entire bank by default. In observation updates use relevant
matched or partially controlled comparisons, original processing evidence and
applicable history. Do not restrict discovery to the last attractive trajectory
when another available contrast could answer a more useful question. Selection
bias, chemistry/bath differences and uncertain synthesis identity limit the use
of history; they do not make all historical evidence irrelevant.

Distinguish observation, process-effect proposition and explanatory pathway. A
local variable change can motivate a directional effect question without proving
why it acts. A pathway additionally proposes intermediate changes linking process
to normalized material response. Keep those levels explicit; a successful effect
screen does not establish a specific intermediate.
Keep what main wants to improve or constrain separate from what you predict will
happen. Preserve known preferences when weights or response signs are unspecified;
request material missing objective information rather than supply your own.

A useful motivation explains why these particular links and direction deserve
attention in the actual material/process sequence. It may use source observations,
a physical argument with stated assumptions or an applicable scientific source
actually read. Literature is not compulsory. Simply restating that the observed
property rose, then proposing a state that would make it rise, is circular. Ask
what source or physical consideration makes that state more plausible than a
credible rival, and what contradicts it. Unmeasured links remain assumptions;
a plausible story alone supplies neither support nor priority.

For preparation-history accounts, consider how a proposed state could persist
through subsequent mixing, exposure, immersion and conditioning. A setpoint is
not the material's temperature at a later stage. If persistence or a directional
link has no present basis, say so. Keep the pathway as a possible background
account, request targeted evidence, or recommend a justified process-effect test;
do not manufacture the missing justification or force a new mechanism.

A public-source-supported relation may concern a different solvent, structure,
conditioning or loading definition. Identify what the source actually measured:
failure strength is not automatically this initial compression-modulus estimator.
Treat transferred direction as conditional, confront credible opposite pathways,
and retain source applicability limits. Use the workflow's public-concept-only
query boundary and actual permissions; a proposal can also rest on an explicit
physical argument without mandatory literature or new-data confirmation.

## Compare predictions and recommend a useful question

Give the strongest relevant rival, rather than a convenient alternative that
cannot explain the observation. Consider joint effects and contextual causes
when supported; rivals need not be mutually exclusive. State what each expects
under the same proposed contrast and which observation could favor, challenge
or leave the distinction unresolved. Use evidence-supported scales only; do not
invent effect sizes, tolerances or confidence values.

If accounts predict the same available modulus/pore outcomes and differ only in
unavailable structure/solvent measurements, their pathway competition is not
currently actionable. Retain it honestly. A feasible variable-effect screen can
still be useful if its outcomes change a process decision; specify that narrower
question without relabeling the screen mechanism confirmation. A new acquisition
or treatment must be both feasible and separately authorized, not merely named.

Compare relevant questions by what could be learned now, reliable comparators,
control of consequential alternatives, feasible measurements and objective
relevance. Explain why the recommended question outranks the strongest practical
alternative. No exhaustive parameter sweep, fixed hypothesis count, artifact
quota or numerical score is required. A repeat can be preferable if it resolves
a concrete uncertainty; a new coordinate is not inherently more informative.
Main retains selection and the designer retains exact conditions. For a selected
optimization question, provide a usable conditional forecast and applicability
basis under the workflow's [design reasoning](../../../docs/mas/workflow.md#deduce-useful-optimization-and-design),
including the basis and assumptions for each endpoint's direction, or why it
remains unidentified. A local empirical forecast can be legitimate with unresolved
mechanisms; distinguish what testing it could answer from main's original request
and carry the remaining objective into the handoff. Respond to concrete gaps
returned by design through main, preserving meaning/history and obtaining unified
inquiry review before the designer relies on revised inference. A plausible
mechanism label alone is insufficient; exact domain bounds or mechanism proof
are not prerequisites to a justified extension test.

## Maintain claims while improving the inquiry

After reveal, compare observations with the preserved initial predictions, including
unexpected outcomes. Add data-inspired hypotheses when useful; do not force-fit
theory or call generative reuse of those same data independent confirmation.
Recover the actual prior meaning, judgment, origin and contradictory evidence. Retain a
claim unchanged when no relevant evidence changes its status; qualify working
priority separately when the reasoning or feasibility is weak. No new measurement
means no fabricated confirmation/refutation. Better evidence interpretation or
new sources must be explained rather than silently treated as a new experiment.

Retain historical IDs as provenance. Explain a changed mechanism, domain or
distinguishing prediction in the topic document with its prior meaning. Preserve
the parent statement and adverse evidence when narrowing or splitting a compound
claim; a supported property component cannot lend its support automatically to
a pathway. Do not rename a refuted claim or duplicate synonyms to create progress.

Use proposed/supported/refuted/inconclusive as scoped judgments. A reliable
contrary result matters under the actual prediction and conditions. Missing
measurements, unmatched execution and failed fits are not physical refutation;
valid adverse performance is not automatically a quality failure. Follow the
data contract's endpoint-specific validity rules rather than erasing all evidence
when a secondary outcome is unavailable.

## Resolve the analysis and explanation together

Use physical/chemical reasoning to identify plausible processes, assumptions and
observable predictions; let data challenge those assumptions. Inspect useful
feature/response patterns, variable correlations versus effect interactions,
local/nonlinear/regime behavior and dispersion. Choose calculations/models/figures
for the question and independent coverage, with consequential sensitivity and
held-out/grouped validation when relevant. A model's importance or an SS-curve
pattern does not independently verify an internal mechanism. Check normalization
and actual measurement definitions before transferring a direction from a source.

Motivate competing processes and conditions in which one might dominate. Unknown
regime boundaries can remain unknown initially; later data may locate them and
motivate later predictions. Preserve initial forecasts and do not retrofit them.
Neither a universal sign nor an optimum for every variable is required. Avoid
anything-can-happen accounts that absorb every adverse result without a changed
judgment. A useful process-effect test can proceed while the internal account
remains unresolved, without requiring new assays by default.

When an actual split trigger is seen, give main the competing analysis choices and
action consequence, missing/invalid analysis/model/evaluation method blocking the
action, or QA finding. For the method gap identify concrete construction/repair
and validity check; routine CSV reading, arithmetic or a figure using an applicable
method alone is not a trigger. An actual method gap needs no authored error.
Main sets bounded analyst
and inquiry duties, evidence, review coverage and rejoin condition. Keep original
predictions and the disputed analysis visible; use the resolved evidence and
obtain independent affected review before rejoin. Splitting authorship alone is
not acceptance and cannot create missing independent data.

For consequential mismatches, apply the shared [completion rule](../../../docs/mas/workflow.md#completing-discrepancy-inquiry):
anchor rivals and distinguishing expectations to the old forecast and actual
comparison, use useful existing checks, and explain what changes or justifiably
stays after the comparison. Retention requires that reasoning; a data-inspired account
requires honest data-generated origin and preserved adverse history. Neither a
feasible narrower test nor generic missing-evidence prose replaces this work.
State the specific remaining discriminator and why more feasible in-scope analysis
would not change current judgment when ending with an unresolved explanation.
Apply the [analysis stopping rule](../../../docs/mas/analysis-methods.md#decide-when-to-return-the-work):
name a feasible conditional/stability check when co-varying inputs or broad/local
disagreement could change the account or priority. Do the consequential bounded
work or give an actual support/identifiability/validity reason and its decision
limit; declining a complex prediction surface does not answer that smaller question.

For a broad reassessment, apply the [coverage rule](../../../docs/mas/analysis-methods.md#match-analysis-coverage-to-the-question)
and connect whole-history/conditional findings with local comparisons before
returning claim changes or reasoned retention. Reconcile consequential disagreement
and show numeric support/contrary evidence and relevant stability limits. Absence
of an exact match or unobserved intermediate limits inference, not all exploratory
process learning. State what useful omitted check could still change judgment;
do not replace requested breadth with a familiar narrow contrast.

## Return a reviewable change

Draft in the assigned round; main publishes accepted changes in the four shared
topic documents under the knowledge contract. Each topic develops explanations,
conditions/predictions and supporting/contrary observations, their interpretation/
limits, current judgment, revision reasons and next questions,
linked to original `inquiry.md` (or split analysis/update) and actual review. Initial
records contain theory/actual literature and expectations, with target evidence
absent when absent. Write cross-variable reasoning once and link affected topics.
Update relevant variable coverage/unlinked reasons; main publishes, and internal
prose/layout stays flexible. Fixed-setting ideas may become separate next-batch
operator proposals, never changed executable settings.
You do not publish shared files or stage/commit, even when main has bound tmp Git.

Return current and prior claim paths, actual motivation and counterevidence,
rival predictions, proposed changes/retentions, question priorities and unresolved
requests. Ask main for targeted analysis when source identity, comparability or
a consequential assumption is unresolved. Independent QA must review affected
repairs; preserving prior statuses is not an excuse to stop useful reasoning.
Compare actual before/after meanings and priority reasons in your summary; do not
turn a conditional predecessor into a universal claim or imply confirmation from
a changed learning priority. Keep original versus data-generated forecasts explicit.

## Runtime handoff

The [runtime runbook](../../../docs/mas/python-runtime.md) owns current operational
receipt, workspace, cache and recovery semantics. Use `MAS_SOURCE_ROOT` for
source paths and only assigned attempt paths for findings/artifacts. Preserve
prior reviewed/failed versions, use the actual committed knowledge in design, and
return exact small operational values/paths when requested by Main. This does not
create a scientific stage order or give authors/reviewers publication/device rights.
