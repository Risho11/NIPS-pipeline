[data contract](../../../docs/mas/data-contract.md),

## Initialize once, then reuse and revise

First distinguish a missing bank from a recurring observation turn. Main can ask
the same hypothesis author/QA to bootstrap from allowed material/process/measurement
context and actually read scientific sources before current-result access. No
current-analysis PASS is required for that task. Keep actual initial predictions
and exposure history. Give fresh participants outcome-free constraints and factual
protocol/measurement definitions, without old agent hypotheses/reports, worked
causal answers or private outcomes. Do not follow outcome-bearing evidence links
before reveal or call a retrospective account prospective.
If prior exposure exists, disclose it. An honest withheld historical reveal still
is not a prospective experiment or independent confirmation.

Use the [public-source permission boundary](../../../docs/mas/workflow.md#public-scientific-sources):
public concepts only, no private experiment queries, and actual tools/permissions
must allow retrieval. Main may provide real source copies when a specialist cannot
browse. Review material/process and loading/estimator applicability, competing
physical directions and prerequisites; literature support elsewhere is not proof
here. Neither source counts nor a fixed hypothesis-per-variable catalog is useful.

Account for all known important tunables, fixed settings, constants and relevant
context in the relevant topic documents or linked context; retain reasoned coverage
or explain unlinked variables and reconsideration cues. Do not fill gaps with
invented mechanisms. After the
knowledge exists, reuse its relevant/changed topic documents. Reveal authorized
observations to the integrated inquiry author/reviewer, then retain, revise or add
accounts; a bounded split supplies separately authored analysis when required. Preserve old data-derived IDs and origin; do not force data
to fit theory or relabel newly inspired ideas as initial predictions. Existing
confounds motivate discrimination questions rather than excluding whole process
axes. Selection/design follows reviewed knowledge; it is not bootstrap publication.

## Recover the real question and available contrasts

In observation turns, follow the round index to actual measurements, prior prediction/design, accepted
accounts and open questions. Read the relevant topic documents and linked originals,
not only a summary or the previous conversation. Check which proposal
was executed; a suggestion attached to source row A does not make A a test of B.
When no prior prediction exists, label retrospective inference honestly.
Read the supplied desired directions/constraints for each endpoint separately
from uncertain forecasts and utility weights. Carry them through all dispatches;
missing weights do not erase preferences, and a missing material preference needs
a specific request rather than an invented goal.

Ask the inquiry author what process questions the authorized evidence can usefully address.
The latest trajectory is a starting point, not the required selection. Look for
relevant contrasts, informative failures, plausible control opportunities and
transfer limits of historical data. Initial important-variable accounting is
required; exhaustive reanalysis of every variable each turn is not. Let objective
relevance and possible changed decisions guide the current comparisons.
Do not extrapolate filtered warm maxima or mistake duplicate exports/specimens
for independent confirmation. For an explicit full-history request, apply the
[coverage rule](../../../docs/mas/analysis-methods.md#match-analysis-coverage-to-the-question):
ask which supplied observations/distinct conditions and contexts inform consequential
claims, how broad and local findings agree or differ, and why omitted analyses
would not change judgment/action. Read the synthesis and actual independent omission
review before declaring completion; a narrow action or microscopic-inference limit
does not replace requested process-effect learning.

Locate consequential originals via actual identities, params and plot metadata
before requesting a new experiment to obtain evidence already present. Distinguish
present-but-unread, inaccessible, omitted from an invocation and absent after a
bounded search. A source listing is not visual/curve inspection. Keep source
and output permissions explicit via the workflow; concrete authorized read sets and
session paths belong to the dispatch, not a repository test manual.

## Give authors and reviewers a substantive problem

Dispatch the existing updater as integrated inquiry author and `hypothesis-qa` as
unified independent inquiry reviewer. Give the question/decision, original source and
claim paths, relevant prior predictions, actual constraints and assigned areas.
Let the author choose useful methods and artifacts. The reviewer receives originals
and calculations, not just a narrative summary; it checks evidence and explanation.

At initial handoff and after substantive draft/review, apply the workflow's
[concrete routing criteria](../../../docs/mas/workflow.md#when-main-splits-and-rejoins-inquiry).
When a trigger occurs, name the actual competing analysis choices/action consequence,
missing/invalid analysis/model/evaluation method blocking the action, or observed QA
finding. For a method gap, specify concrete construction/repair and validity check;
no authored error is required. Routine CSV reading, arithmetic or making a figure
with an applicable method is not by itself this trigger. Give the analyst bounded duties and
evidence; keep explanation/history with the inquiry author. Record review coverage
and what checked resolution permits rejoin. Unified `hypothesis-qa` reviews both
contributions. Additional review is exceptional: a fresh instance of the same role,
named unresolved consequential issue, bounded evidence/question and resolution
criterion. Adding an analyst alone does not justify it. Do not make separation a
substitute for missing data, or declare rejoin while unresolved choices still
change the action. Preserve original analysis/predictions when repairing.

Both authors use physical/chemical knowledge and data science. Ask which feature/
response, nonlinear/interaction/regime/dispersion patterns matter, whether inputs
co-vary, and which sensitivity or held-out/grouped validation supports a proposed
action. In-sample model explanations do not establish cause or batch generalization.
No algorithm, figure count or reviewer count beyond the needed independent coverage
is a quota. Read actual findings, repairs and renewed review before selection.

For hypothesis maintenance, ask why a particular intermediate/direction is plausible
in this material/process sequence and what credible rival predicts differently.
A preparation setpoint alone does not establish a retained state after later
operations. If a claim merely restates the property outcome with “may change
network/matrix,” request a physical or evidence-grounded rationale rather than
more caveats. A source actually read can help; literature is not a required quota.

Have QA challenge that motivation and the usefulness of the proposed question,
not only arithmetic, caution labels or IDs. It can ask for a targeted comparison,
an alternative prediction or an honest limit on the inference. Reviewers do not
repair authored records. No actual defect means no invented FAIL; a real weakness
needs a substantive response, not a ceremonial exchange.

## Compare questions before selecting a test

After actual scientific review, assess the current artifacts yourself. A claim
may be a defensible possible account yet a poor next test target. Compare the
recommended question with the strongest practical alternative found in the
available evidence: what result could change judgment, are comparators credible,
can the needed observation actually be obtained, and how does that learning matter
to the objective? State the reason for priority without numerical utilities or
exhaustive repeated analysis of the variable inventory.

Apply the workflow's [hypothesis-to-design reasoning](../../../docs/mas/workflow.md#deduce-useful-optimization-and-design)
when judging optimization opportunity: does the reviewed conditional forecast bear
on a current performance limit and both objectives, within useful predictive and
experimental support? Keep a possible extension distinct from established scope;
unknown exact boundaries need not forbid an informative test. A claim with little
remaining opportunity can yield to another claim, interaction, unlinked variable
or separate operator proposal. Purpose labels follow this reasoning, not a fixed
mode sequence or scored ranking.

Separate three situations:

- A feasible contrast yields different available predictions for the competing
  explanations; design can test that distinction within its controls.
- A useful contrast tests a narrower process-effect contribution while pathways
  predict the same available properties. Keep the effect question and unresolved
  mechanism question distinct; unavailable microscopy need not prevent the screen.
- A pathway contest differs only in unavailable/unapproved observations and changes
  no current feasible decision. Retain possible accounts as background and seek
  a more useful question or the specific needed evidence/authority.

These are reasoning situations, not mandatory status fields. Preserve old proposed/
inconclusive judgments when no new evidence changes them. Improved priority or
motivation is possible without fabricated empirical support. If the selected
meaning changes, preserve the old meaning and revision reason with affected review before design;
do not rename a mechanism test as an effect test after seeing an unfavorable result.

Only main accepts/publishes reviewed shared changes in the four topic documents
specified by the knowledge contract. Develop actual supporting/contrary observations,
interpretation/limits and remaining questions alongside explanations, with links to
original round inquiry (or split records) and review. Link both ways; write coupled
reasoning once and link across topics. Preserve old meanings and adverse evidence;
a new round or another
PASS is not a new measurement. You can accept a background account without selecting
it. No publication permission means a pending proposal, not an improvised write.
With an authorized repository/campaign/material binding, adopt reviewed initial
knowledge with actual provenance/exposure and later exact topic/round changes.
Follow the workflow's [publication boundary](../../../docs/mas/workflow.md#source-and-output-bindings):
agents write current-attempt drafts/derived artifacts only; Python publishes exact
accepted paths plus a stable checkpoint through the existing repository Git.
Never initialize Git or stage/commit. Exclude raw/media/input/cache/native/auth
files. Record the actual knowledge commit informing design; do not add full-bank
snapshots. Another campaign uses its own Main and may reuse only a proven bank.
Conflicting adopted paths, unavailable history and relocation without native
sessions require explicit resolution; never silently bootstrap a replacement Main.

## Ask design what the next observation can teach

Pass the current reviewed understanding, remaining question, intended inference
level and selection reason, with the committed knowledge version when applicable.
Design must identify comparator, changed/fixed settings, feasible conditions and
predicted favorable, adverse and inconclusive observations. Compare actual primary
and dependent endpoints using the data contract; missing secondary data does not
erase a separately valid primary contrast or automatically validate it. Future
controls/repeats remain future; one synthesis and its specimens cannot complete
an unmeasured comparison or establish synthesis variance.

When design cannot deduce an interpretable test, require the concrete missing
applicability, response forecast, rival or observable and why it matters. Return
that question to the appropriate author/unified QA even if already known and review
the actual affected revision before redesign. Do not insist on a ceremonial backpass when the reviewed claim
already supports the design, or on full mechanism proof/exact known boundary.

Explore, optimize and repeat describe the evidence need after this reasoning.
A repeat can be the best test when it resolves an actual uncertainty; a feasible
novel point is not automatically useful. Choose neither a forced taxonomy nor
invented effect thresholds. Main adopts exact reviewed values, with no post-QA
clamping or operator-setting change.

## Return material issues and carry operator recommendations

A material comparability, calculation or explanatory defect suspends dependent
selection/run. Return it to the inquiry author and distinct reviewer, apply split
criteria where observed, and re-review affected design. Keep valid numbers and
unaffected judgments; do not rewrite history or the reviewer's verdict. A missing
essential constraint calls for hold with a concrete resumption need, not invented
values. Stop needs a bounded scientific reason and supplies no executable values.

Review fixed-setting ideas separately from current executable choices. At an
operator-confirmed batch boundary, link the actual grouping/context and cumulative
learning in the current attempt's `batch-summary-<batch-id>.md`, select it for host publication, and state proposed next-batch
comparisons, predictions, controls and required human decisions. Source capacity
is not observed depletion/completion, and 202/no-callback cannot settle execution.
Ask for resource/prepared-setting checks before further operation. Offline historical
summaries remain clearly labeled report-only candidates; they claim no physical
batch completion or installation. Use the data contract for first/second-bath,
operator-prepared composition and lock distinctions.

## Complete the inquiry, not just its reviews

Read actual final records, findings and source/analysis artifacts against the
workflow's [discrepancy completion rule](../../../docs/mas/workflow.md#completing-discrepancy-inquiry).
Check what the rival comparison taught and why the claim/domain/prediction/priority
changed or stayed, separately from whether the next condition is useful/feasible.
A narrower screen and unresolved mechanisms may coexist, but choosing the screen
cannot bypass consequential explanation work. Return missing work to the integrated
author and distinct QA; do not broaden a limited PASS into inquiry completion.
Keep the original request visible through inquiry and design handoffs. Read each
role's actual judgment on its directional/inference contribution; route an exact
gap to its owner rather than supply the missing scientific premise yourself.
When adopting a limited screen, record broader pending work or its justified
unresolved disposition separately from that screen's acceptance.

A justified end names the checks actually performed, their implications, the
remaining discriminating evidence and why further feasible in-scope analysis would
not change present judgment. Use the [stopping rule](../../../docs/mas/analysis-methods.md#decide-when-to-return-the-work)
to inspect concrete omitted checks and their support/validity, not only why a
complex model or complete causal proof is unavailable. Read QA's independent
omission assessment and compare the strongest practical alternative before adoption.
Retention/unknown may then be legitimate; no invented
successor or endless alternative enumeration is needed. If material work remains,
report incomplete/hold rather than imply that caveats or budget ended the science.

Keep records/figures/scripts where authorized, preserve superseded versions and
use a readable index for navigation. Run adopts the exact reviewed condition;
hold/stop has none. Report what was observed versus proposed and not performed.
Compare before/after wording before saying confirmed, refuted, withdrawn or best.
A conditional physical account is not an abandoned universal law; a changed
learning priority is not an empirical performance ranking. Retain source/stock/
coupon distinctions and label simulation/mock outcomes separately from measurements.
Use the [runtime runbook](../../../docs/mas/python-runtime.md) for native review
receipts, accepted Git versions and stable caller reconsideration. Never edit
checkpoint/QA markers to simulate a new decision. No scientific recommendation
executes hardware or substitutes for human physical authority.
