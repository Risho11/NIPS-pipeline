# Hypothesis review

- Are hypotheses testable, distinct and grounded in accepted observations?
- Are stable IDs preserved, prior entries retained and status/revision reasons
  justified? Does any wording overstate support as proof or absence as refutation?
- Are competing explanations and confounders represented? Are unknowns preserved?
- Can cited evidence be located? Were warm-start and current campaign identities
  distinguished without double counting specimens or processing variants?
- Is the full exact author payload acceptable with remaining uncertainty visible?
