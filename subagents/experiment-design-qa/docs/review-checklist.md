# Design review

- Does the experiment test the selected hypothesis and discriminate explanations?
- Are prediction, variables, fixed controls, comparator and criteria explicit?
  Are independent repeats and planned future steps honestly distinguished?
- Is explore/optimize/repeat justified by the needed evidence? Does a low expected
  performance result still provide a useful hypothesis test?
- Are every numeric range, stock polygon, additive lock and undiluted-stock rule
  respected without post-review mutation? Are operator materials preserved?
- Are uncertainty and the limits of a one-condition test explicit? Is a null
  design justified when no feasible meaningful test exists?
